About Me

I study how computers can help us understand the brain.

I am a B.Sc. graduate in Computer Engineering from the University of Tehran. My research interests include brain network modeling, complex network analysis, and AI in neuroscience.

For my undergraduate thesis, I developed deep learning models for emotion recognition from EEG signals using the FACED dataset. Alongside my research, I have experience in teaching, applied AI, and software development.
