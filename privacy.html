Privacy and optional visit analytics

Last updated: 31 October 2026

This academic website is operated by Amir Rajabi. Contact: arajabi@aut.ac.ir.

Your choice

Detailed analytics starts only after you choose “Accept”. Before a choice, this site sends one anonymous count request containing only an empty object, without cookies or a visitor identifier. The database keeps only a daily total, not an individual record. Rejecting analytics stops optional detailed tracking and future optional count requests from this browser; it does not affect access to the website or CV. Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals disable both features.

Anonymous daily totals

When optional counting is not rejected or blocked by a privacy signal, a page load increments a daily total. This table has only a date and a count: no IP address, exact visit time, identifier, device information, location, referral information or individual browsing history. The network provider necessarily processes the incoming connection and may keep its own security logs separately. The daily totals do not distinguish people, repeat visits or bots. Because they cannot be linked to a visitor, individual contributions to those totals cannot later be identified or removed.

What is recorded after acceptance

  • A random visitor identifier and a separate visit identifier, visit/last-update timestamps, and the version of the notice accepted.
  • Your connection IP address, approximate country/region/city and network ASN as supplied by Cloudflare. These can describe a VPN or proxy rather than your location.
  • User-agent string, inferred browser/operating-system/device category, browser language, time zone, screen dimensions and viewport dimensions.
  • The referring website’s origin/domain, when available. Referrer paths, fragments and query strings are omitted.
  • Page views, sections seen, maximum scroll depth, CV-link clicks, contact-email clicks and clicks on the existing external academic/profile links. Email-link events record the action, not an email you write. CV-link clicks do not prove a download completed.
  • Approximate elapsed, visible and active time. “Active” requires a visible, focused page and recent interaction; the content of keys or text input is not recorded.
  • Available performance measurements: navigation/response/load timings, first contentful paint, largest contentful paint and cumulative layout shift.

Names, precise GPS location, personal accounts, passwords, form contents, keystroke contents, camera/microphone data, canvas fingerprints and browsing history are not collected by this analytics system. An IP address does not identify a person reliably. Blocking scripts or closing a tab can prevent delivery or make timing incomplete.

Purpose and storage

The purpose is understanding interest in this academic profile and improving the website. Records are stored in a private Cloudflare D1 database configured with EU jurisdiction, accessed by a Cloudflare Worker. Analytics records have no public read endpoint and are not committed to the public GitHub repository. There is no automatic expiration: records, including IP addresses, remain until the site owner deletes them or a visitor requests deletion. Cloudflare’s own backup and platform retention policies also apply.

Analytics data is not sold or used for advertising or cross-site tracking. Cloudflare processes the collection requests and stores the data; GitHub hosts the website. Infrastructure/security logging by these providers is separate from these analytics features and may occur regardless of your analytics choice. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy and GitHub’s privacy statement.

Change your choice or delete recorded visits

Use “Manage Cookies” in the notice, or “Privacy” in the website footer, to open your preferences. You can separately choose anonymous daily counts and detailed analytics, use “Save preferences”, or use “Reject optional analytics” to disable both. “Delete my recorded visits” is available while your visitor identifier is still available in this browser. Deletion removes visits and events linked to that identifier from the active analytics database. If you have already cleared browser storage, use the contact address above; identification of old records may be limited. A failed deletion request is shown as an error rather than reported as successful.

Your accept/reject preference is saved in this browser for up to 180 days so that you are not asked on every visit. After acceptance, a random visitor ID is stored until you reject analytics, request deletion, or clear site data. This is browser storage, not a third-party tracking cookie.

Limitations

Reports describe only consenting, observable visits. They are not a complete count of all visitors. Client events can be blocked, delayed, lost, duplicated or fabricated; they are not proof of a person’s identity or actions. Approximate location and inferred device details may be wrong.
